How to read it

Leading: beating the market and still gaining ground.

Emerging: still behind the market, but improving. Early rotation candidates.

Weakening: ahead of the market, but losing momentum. Watch for exits.

Lagging: behind the market and still falling back.

Categories usually move clockwise: emerging, leading, weakening, lagging. Dot size is the number of stocks. Hover a dot to see its path over the last sessions; click it to open the category.
